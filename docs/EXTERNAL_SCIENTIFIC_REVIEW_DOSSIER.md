## 1. Objective of this Dossier

This document provides external scientific evaluators with a complete, transparent, and reproducible technical basis to assess the scientific validity, empirical error bounds, and applicability of the **BlueCarbon Registry MRV Accounting Methodology**.

Before any carbon figure generated by this platform is presented as an accredited, tradable carbon asset, it must undergo formal review by independent domain specialists. This dossier formalizes that review process.

## 2. Core Methodological Equations

### 2.1 Net Carbon Sequestration Model

$$C_{\text{net}} = \Big[ (C_{\text{gross}} - D_{\text{CH}_4} - D_{\text{leakage}}) \times (1 - B_{\text{reversal}}) \times (1 - U_{\text{discount}}) \Big]$$

Where:

- $C_{\text{gross}} = A \times T \times \Big( (R_{\text{bio}} \times S_{\text{rate}}) + R_{\text{soil}} \Big)$
- $A$: Project area in hectares (computed via spherical geodesic polygon integration).
- $T$: Elapsed duration in years between baseline planting date and monitoring date:
  $$T = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{MonitoringTimestamp} - \text{StartTimestamp}}{365.25 \times 86400 \times 1000}, 2\right)$$
- $R_{\text{bio}}$: Ecosystem biomass sequestration rate ($7.80\text{ tCO}_2\text{e/ha/yr}$ for mangroves).
- $S_{\text{rate}}$: Sapling survival rate fraction ($0.0 \dots 1.0$) measured via permanent allometric plots.
- $R_{\text{soil}}$: Mineral soil organic carbon accretion rate ($5.90\text{ tCO}_2\text{e/ha/yr}$ for mangroves).
- $D_{\text{CH}_4}$: Methane emission deduction ($5.42\text{ tCO}_2\text{e/ha/yr}$ applied when water salinity $< 18.0\text{ ppt}$; $0$ when $\ge 18.0\text{ ppt}$).
- $D_{\text{leakage}}$: Activity displacement deduction ($10.0\%$ default when displacement is identified; $0\%$ within protected reserves).
- $B_{\text{reversal}}$: Mandatory **15.0%** non-permanence risk buffer withholding allocated to an uncredited reserve pool.
- $U_{\text{discount}}$: Scientific uncertainty discount based on verified evidence tiers:
  - **Tier 1 (Literature Default):** $10.0\%$ deduction.
  - **Tier 2 (Site Calibrated):** $5.0\%$ deduction (requires verified ground-truth sampling or allometric survey).
  - **Tier 3 (Soil Cores & Allometry):** $3.0\%$ deduction (requires validated depth-stratified sediment cores and permanent allometric census plots).

## 3. Scientific Rationale for Model Architecture

### 3.1 Decoupling of Vegetative Biomass from Soil Accretion

A foundational design choice in this methodology is the mathematical separation between living tree biomass accumulation and sediment mineral carbon trapping:

1. **Biomass Accumulation ($R_{\text{bio}} \times S_{\text{rate}}$):** Directly dependent on sapling establishment, crown expansion, and tree survival ($S_{\text{rate}}$). If survival drops to $50\%$, vegetative accumulation scales down proportionally.
2. **Soil Carbon Accretion ($R_{\text{soil}}$):** In tidal coastal wetlands, autochthonous root exudates and allochthonous sediment trapping occur across the hydrodynamically connected wetland basin. Even when seedling density is sparse during early establishment years, intertidal mineral sediment deposition continues as long as tidal exchange is unobstructed. In Tier 3, this rate is calibrated using site-specific $^{210}\text{Pb}$-dated sediment cores.

### 3.2 Methane Emission Suppression Threshold

Following IPCC (2013) Wetlands Supplement Chapter 4 and Poffenbarger et al. (2011), high sulfate concentrations in seawater ($\text{salinity} \ge 18\text{ ppt}$) favor sulfate-reducing bacteria over methanogens, driving biogenic methane emissions to negligible levels. When salinity drops below $18\text{ ppt}$ (e.g., upper river estuaries during monsoon discharge), the model applies the standard IPCC default deduction of $193.7\text{ kg CH}_4\text{/ha/yr} \times 28\text{ GWP-100} = 5.42\text{ tCO}_2\text{e/ha/yr}$.
